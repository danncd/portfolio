Anime List is a macOS app for browsing anime and manga through AniList and organizing titles into personal lists.

## How it works

The app uses React and TypeScript inside Electron. It gets title information from AniList's GraphQL API and saves personal lists locally, without an account or an app backend.

It includes seasonal browsing, search, title details, favorites, and custom lists.
